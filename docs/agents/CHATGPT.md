## How this doc lifts the five pillars

- **Efficiency:** the paste block forces the budget block and the one-action rule, so a reply names what it will not read.
- **Accuracy:** the label set and the scope rule stop ChatGPT from turning a gap into a fact. Founder acceptance stays separate from ChatGPT's reconciliation.
- **Speed:** fingerprint-first and DEAD END naming stop repeated loops across sessions.
- **Growth:** a defect becomes a dated rule in section 8 of `docs/ULTRATHINK_KNOWLEDGE.md`. ChatGPT proposes it; the founder accepts it.
- **Knowledge:** the paste block points at the repo docs. The skill inventory and gaps are in section 7, so ChatGPT does not treat a gap as a skill.
